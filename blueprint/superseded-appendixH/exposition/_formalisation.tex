Every result below has been formalised in Lean~4 against Mathlib \texttt{v4.31.0}. The
development compiles with no unproved goal, and each of its theorems has been checked to
depend on no axiom beyond Lean's own \texttt{propext}, \texttt{Classical.choice} and
\texttt{Quot.sound}. The proofs given here are accordingly sketches: each says what carries
the argument and which earlier statement it rests on, and leaves the verification of the
steps to that development. Table~\ref{tab:doubling_formal} records the correspondence
statement by statement, and marks the three places where a clause of the appendix is a
property of the backward chain as a stochastic process, which the development does not
model.
