Since $p_*>1$ the map $t\mapsto t^{-p_*}$ is decreasing and integrable at $+\infty$ with
$\int_m^{+\infty}t^{-p_*}dt=m^{1-p_*}/(p_*-1)$, so comparing the sum with the integral gives
\eqref{eq:doubling_powertail} and, with $m\geq1$, the bounds $1\leq1+\xi_m\leq p_*$. Summing
\eqref{eq:doubling_decay} over $j\geq m$ and inserting those two bounds gives the first pair of
\eqref{eq:doubling_tailratio}; dividing that pair by \eqref{eq:doubling_decay} at $j=m$ gives
the second. Finally $p_*>1$ sends the upper bound $c_2p_*m^{1-p_*}/(p_*-1)$ on $L(m)$ to $0$
and the lower bound $c_1m/\bigl(c_2(p_*-1)\bigr)$ on $L(m)/\lambda_m$ to $+\infty$.
