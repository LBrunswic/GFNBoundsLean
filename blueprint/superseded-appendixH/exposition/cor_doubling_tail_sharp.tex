By \eqref{eq:doubling_powertail}, $\sum_{j\geq m}j^{-p_*}=\frac{m^{1-p_*}}{p_*-1}(1+\xi_m)$
with $0\leq\xi_m\leq(p_*-1)/m$. Given $t>0$, Theorem~\ref{theo:doubling_sharp} supplies an
$m_t$ beyond which $|u_j-C|\leq t$, and summing \eqref{eq:doubling_decay} in the sharpened
form $C-t\leq u_j\leq C+t$ over $j\geq m$ brackets $L(m)$ between $(C\mp t)\sum_{j\geq
m}j^{-p_*}$. Hence every limit point of $(p_*-1)m^{p_*-1}L(m)$ lies in $[C-t,C+t]$, and $t$
being arbitrary the sequence converges to $C$: the first line of
\eqref{eq:doubling_tail_sharp}. Dividing by $u_m=\lambda_m m^{p_*}\rightarrow C>0$ gives the
second.
