Since $m\geq1$ gives $\varepsilon(m)=c/(m+1)\leq c/2<1$, the substitution
$\varepsilon(j)=c/(j+1)$ in \eqref{eq:doubling_cut} followed by division by
$1-\varepsilon(m)>0$ turns the cut balance into the average \eqref{eq:doubling_avg}, whose
weights $q_m$ are positive on $W(m)$. Multiplying by $m^{p}>0$ and writing
$\lambda_j=u_j\,j^{-p}$ rescales that average into the first line of
\eqref{eq:doubling_uavg}, with weights $w_m(j)=q_m(j)(m/j)^{p}$; their sum is
\eqref{eq:doubling_R} at $\alpha=0$, where $\Phi_0(j)=j^{-p}$.
