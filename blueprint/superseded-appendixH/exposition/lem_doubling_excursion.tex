From a ladder state the chain moves to $2j\geq j$ or to $j-1$, the doubling edge being
absent on the truncation when $2j>K$ and the decrement then carrying probability~$1$; in
either chain $X_{n+1}\geq X_n-1$ at every step issued from a ladder state, and started at a
ladder state the chain visits only ladder states before $\sigma$, the state $s_f$ being
entered from $s_0$ alone. The inequality is vacuous on $\{\sigma=+\infty\}$. On
$\{\sigma<+\infty\}$ let $n_*$ attain the maximum: each of the $\sigma-n_*$ steps from $n_*$
to $\sigma$ lowers the chain by at most $1$ and ends at $X_\sigma=0$, so
$\sigma\geq\sigma-n_*\geq\max_{0\leq n\leq\sigma}X_n$.
