Every state visited before the exit is $\geq2\ell$, so $|b(Y_n)|\leq H/(2\ell)\leq\gamma^2/12$
and every factor of the product lies in $[\tfrac12,\tfrac32]$. Write
$\Psi(z):=\mathbb E\bigl(\prod_{n<N_\ell}(1+b(Y_n))\mid Y_0=z\bigr)$ and let $\varphi_i$ and
$\underline\varphi_i$ be its supremum and infimum over the dyadic block $I_i(\ell)$, so that
$\varphi_0=\underline\varphi_0=1$, the product being empty there.

Fix $i\geq1$ and let $\varsigma$ be the exit time of $I_i(\ell)$. By
Lemma~\ref{lem:doubling_descent}\emph{(1)} the exit lands in $I_{i-1}(\ell)$ and
$\varsigma\leq N_\ell$, so the factors being positive the strong Markov property at $\varsigma$
gives $\Psi(z)=\mathbb E\bigl(\prod_{n<\varsigma}(1+b(Y_n))\,\Psi(Y_\varsigma)\mid Y_0=z\bigr)$.
On $I_i(\ell)$ one has $|b|\leq H2^{-i}/\ell$, so with $\zeta_i:=1+H2^{-i}/\ell$ the
exponential-moment bound of \eqref{eq:doubling_sojourn} gives
$\varphi_i\leq\varphi_{i-1}\bigl(1+c_5H2^{-i}/\ell\bigr)$, and summing the geometric series
over $i$ leaves $\varphi_i\leq e^{c_5H/\ell}$. In the other direction
$(1-x)^{\varsigma}\geq1-\varsigma x$ and the mean bound of \eqref{eq:doubling_sojourn} give
$\underline\varphi_i\geq\underline\varphi_{i-1}\bigl(1-2H2^{-i}/(\gamma^2\ell)\bigr)$, and
$1-x\geq e^{-2x}$ on $[0,\tfrac12]$ turns the product into $e^{-c_5H/\ell}$. The starting
state $m\geq2\ell$ lies in some $I_i(\ell)$, so $\Psi(m)$ lies between the two, which is
\eqref{eq:doubling_product}.
