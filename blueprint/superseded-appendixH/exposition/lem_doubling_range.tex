For $j\geq1$ the value $\varepsilon_{c,s}(j)=c\,(j+1)^{-s}$ is positive, $c$ being positive,
and $j\mapsto(j+1)^{-s}$ is non-increasing, $s$ being non-negative; the supremum over
$j\geq1$ is therefore attained at $j=1$ and equals $c\,2^{-s}$, which is $<1$ by $c<2^s$.
Hence $\varepsilon_{c,s}$ takes its values in $(0,1)$, and by \eqref{eq:doubling_policy} the
decrement edge out of the state $1$ carries $1-c\,2^{-s}>0$.
