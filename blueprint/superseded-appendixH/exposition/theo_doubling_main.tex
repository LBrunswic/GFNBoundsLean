\emph{(1)} Irreducibility on the whole standing range and every row of the table are
Proposition~\ref{prop:doubling_phase}: row \emph{(a)} is its item \emph{(2)}, rows \emph{(b)}
to \emph{(e)} its item \emph{(3)}, which classifies every $c\in(0,2)$ but $1/\ln2$ and denies
positive recurrence throughout $1\leq c<2$, and row \emph{(f)} its item \emph{(1)}. Rows
\emph{(b)} and \emph{(f)} are therefore exactly the positive recurrent members.

\emph{(2)} There $\lambda$ exists and is positive, and $p_*>1$ because $c<1$
(Lemma~\ref{lem:doubling_cramer_root}). Proposition~\ref{prop:doubling_cut} gives the cut
balance at every $m>d$, so Theorem~\ref{theo:doubling_sharp} supplies the limit $C$ and its
dependence on $c$, $d$ and $\lambda_1,\dots,\lambda_d$, and
Corollary~\ref{cor:doubling_tail_sharp} the tail form. That limit is positive because
Theorem~\ref{theo:doubling_decay} confines it to $[c_1,c_2]$, and
Proposition~\ref{prop:doubling_constant} identifies it as \eqref{eq:doubling_constant}.

\emph{(3)} On the standing range $\varepsilon_{c,s}$ is bounded by
$\varepsilon_{\max}<1$ (Lemma~\ref{lem:doubling_range}) and \eqref{eq:doubling_star} holds by
Theorem~\ref{theo:doubling_unbounded}\emph{(1)}; at a positive recurrent member its item
\emph{(2)} then gives \eqref{eq:doubling_inf} and excludes a bounded $S$ at every finite $p$,
the operator acting on each $L^p(\lambda)$ by Lemma~\ref{lem:doubling_operator}\emph{(1)}.
Rows \emph{(b)} and \emph{(f)} have $s\geq1$, so $\gamma$ is finite and
Lemma~\ref{lem:doubling_ramp} extends both conclusions to $p=+\infty$. At $p=2$ the excluded
operator is the one defining $\widehat B$, which is therefore $+\infty$. Finiteness of
$\bar\sigma$ at $s=1$, $c<1$ is Proposition~\ref{prop:doubling_length}, and the inequality with
$c_7$ is Corollary~\ref{cor:doubling_family}.

\emph{(4)} The hypotheses of Proposition~\ref{prop:doubling_unsolvable} are those just
checked, and its item \emph{(2)} is the claim.

\emph{(5)} Lemma~\ref{lem:doubling_truncation_irreducible} makes the truncation at an even
$K\geq d$ a finite irreducible chain on $K+2$ states with a unique $\lambda^K$ and
$\widehat B_K<+\infty$, and $c_8$, $K_0$ are those of
Corollary~\ref{cor:doubling_truncation}\emph{(3)}.
